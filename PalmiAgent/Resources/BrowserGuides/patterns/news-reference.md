# News and Reference

Understand the topic, language, region, and desired date range before browsing.
For weather, establish the exact city or district, dates, local time zone, and units.
For dictionary tasks, establish the language pair, intended meaning, and usage context.
For encyclopedia tasks, distinguish similarly named people, places, and concepts.
Ask the user when an ambiguity changes the answer or the chosen source.

For weather, verify the selected location, forecast period, units, and warning issue time.
For dictionary entries, read the relevant sense, part of speech, pronunciation, and examples.
For encyclopedia claims, examine cited references and identify uncertain or disputed statements.
