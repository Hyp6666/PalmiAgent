# Tickets and entertainment

- Establish the destination city, event or attraction, preferred date, party size, age categories, budget, language, and accessibility needs.
- Confirm the local time zone and whether the requested date refers to the performance, admission, or ticket sale opening.
- Open the consumer entry, observe the current page, and select the correct city or venue before searching.
- Search by the event, artist, film, or attraction name; distinguish actual event results from artist profiles and promotional articles.
- Read the event detail, venue address, start time, duration, age restrictions, identity requirements, and ticket delivery method.
- Compare available dates, sessions, ticket categories, seat views, and admission inclusions using current page evidence.
- Check whether a ticket includes an assigned seat, standing admission, timed entry, or a separate reservation requirement.
- Observe availability and sale status; a listing does not prove tickets are currently purchasable.
- Ask the user to confirm the exact event, date, session, quantity, ticket category, seats, total price, fees, and cancellation terms before committing to a reservation or purchase.
- Use the latest observation identifiers after each navigation, filter change, seat selection, or cart update.
- Treat a seat hold or reservation as time limited; observe the expiry and report it without promising availability.
- Hand off login, verification codes, identity document entry, payment details, interactive seating controls that require direct manipulation, and final payment to the user.
- When admission or ticket delivery requires an app, report that requirement and hand off the app step.
- After the user resumes, observe the page to determine whether payment or booking completed before taking another action.
- Report the confirmed event or attraction, local date and time, venue, quantity, seat or ticket category, paid amount, booking status, ticket delivery instructions, and source link.
- Separate confirmed booking details from unpurchased options and unresolved availability.
