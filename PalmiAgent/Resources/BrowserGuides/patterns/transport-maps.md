# Transport and Maps Workflow

1. Understand whether the user needs a place, a route, a timetable, a fare, or a ride booking.
2. Establish the country, city, precise origin and destination, travel date, local time, and transport mode.
3. Use ask_user when ambiguous addresses, station names, departure times, accessibility needs, or passenger counts change the result.
4. Confirm the supported region of the selected entry before opening it.
5. Observe the page and identify the map search, journey form, station selector, or service alerts.
6. Enter locations separately and choose the matching address or station from suggestions.
7. Check the local time zone and whether the form means depart at, arrive by, or travel now.
8. Apply user constraints such as fewer transfers, walking distance, wheelchair access, luggage, or avoiding tolls.
9. Submit the query and observe fresh route results; open details to inspect transfers, stops, walking sections, and disruptions.
10. Read fares together with ticket type, currency, concessions, zones, and any restrictions.
11. Distinguish estimated travel times and live arrivals from scheduled times; record the displayed update time.
12. Request location permission only when the user wants their current position; verify the returned location against their intent.
13. Use visual observation when the map has no readable route structure, and verify the associated text or station details.
14. Ask for confirmation before booking a ride, purchasing a ticket, changing a reservation, or sharing a location externally.
15. Use handoff for passwords, payment credentials, verification challenges, or required native app actions; resume with a fresh observation after the user returns.
16. Report the selected route or place, local departure and arrival times, transfers, fare basis, relevant alerts, and source URL.
17. State any demonstrated web limitation and whether the requested query or transaction was completed.
