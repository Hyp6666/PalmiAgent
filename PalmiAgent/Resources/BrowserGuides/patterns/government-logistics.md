# Government and Logistics

1. Identify the jurisdiction, service, and desired outcome: public information, eligibility, application progress, parcel tracking, or a utility enquiry.
2. Confirm nationality, residence, travel purpose and dates when relevant; collect only the identifiers needed for the selected task.
3. For parcel tracking, obtain the carrier and tracking number from the user. Preserve leading zeros and distinguish an order reference from a carrier number.
4. Open the public service entry and observe its language, mobile navigation, notices, forms, and account requirements.
5. Select the applicable region and service before reading eligibility, required documents, fees, processing times, or delivery events.
6. Use visible search and form controls with fresh observations after each action. Read result details and linked official instructions.
7. Treat login, identity verification, passwords, one-time codes, payment fields, and document signing as manual handoff steps.
8. Ask the user to confirm the completed details and any fee before submitting an application, booking, shipment, payment, address change, or cancellation.
9. After authorized submission, observe the confirmation and reference number. Do not repeat a submission while its outcome is uncertain.
10. Report the exact status, event time and timezone, service jurisdiction, fees or deadlines, and source URL. Separate estimates from confirmed events.
11. Describe missing data or a blocked service explicitly; never invent a tracking event, application decision, account balance, or successful submission.
