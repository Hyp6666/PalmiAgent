# Shopping workflow

1. Understand the product, purpose, quantity, budget, condition, size, color, and required delivery date.
2. Establish the delivery country, postal code, currency, and preferred marketplace. Use ask_user for missing details affecting the choice.
3. Open the verified consumer entry and observe regional settings, account state, and delivery location.
4. Search with the requested terms or a verified query template. Observe results before applying category, price, condition, and delivery filters.
5. Inspect the exact model, variant, seller, condition, stock, price, shipping charges, taxes, return terms, and estimated delivery.
6. For used goods, inspect photos, defects, seller history, included accessories, collection options, and buyer protection.
7. For price comparisons, follow the retailer offer and verify its total delivered price and availability on the current retailer page.
8. Compare equivalent variants and quantities. Record membership, coupon, subscription, and minimum-spend conditions.
9. Observe again after navigation or changes. Use current element references and inspect unclear variant selections or controls.
10. Ask the user to confirm the item, variant, quantity, seller, delivery address, total price, and delivery method before placing an order or making a binding offer.
11. Use manual handoff for login, account verification, passwords, payment details, and controls requiring direct interaction. Observe again afterward.
12. After an authorized submission, inspect confirmation or order status before reporting success. Inspect order history before repeating an uncertain action.
13. Report selected items, comparable prices, delivery constraints, and source links. For purchases, include visible confirmation details without exposing private account or address data.
