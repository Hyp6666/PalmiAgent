# Food delivery and groceries

Understand whether the user wants prepared meals, groceries, delivery, or collection.
Collect the delivery area, requested time, budget, serving count, dietary needs, and must-have items.
Use only an address the user has provided or explicitly selected; ask when the destination is ambiguous.
Confirm coverage and store opening hours before comparing offers.
Check the current location and delivery mode after opening the consumer entry.
Observe the current page before each action and use identifiers from the latest observation.
Enter the address or postcode through visible controls and inspect the resulting service area.
Search for the requested cuisine, restaurant, or product and compare currently available results.
Read product size, quantity, ingredients, allergens, substitutions, minimum spend, and delivery timing.
Do not infer allergy safety from a category label; report the stated ingredient or allergen information.
Compare the payable total including delivery, service fees, taxes, discounts, and applicable tips.
Add the requested items and inspect the basket after every quantity or option change.
Ask the user to resolve missing dietary choices, substitution preferences, or delivery instructions.
Hand off sign-in, verification challenges, sensitive payment fields, and required app-only steps to the user.
After handoff, observe again and confirm the address, account context, basket, and fulfillment time.
Present the final items, quantities, delivery destination, arrival estimate, and total for confirmation.
Obtain explicit user confirmation before submitting an order or changing an existing order.
Submit only once and inspect the confirmation page before reporting success.
If the page is interrupted after submission, inspect order history before taking another action.
Report the order identifier, merchant, confirmed total, fulfillment time, and consumer page link.
For research-only requests, report current choices and fees with their location and observation time.
State an observed access or app requirement clearly without claiming an order was completed.
