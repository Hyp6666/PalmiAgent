# App Store creative assets — 26.10

The product-page header uses a single abstract pigment form with generous space. Search artwork adds one prominent localized line. These are brand discovery assets; feature demonstrations remain in the screenshots.

Reference: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/asset-best-practices/ and https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/205/ . Apple's static Photoshop templates were inspected for both placements. Export sizes are 3840 × 1646 (header) and 3840 × 2560 (search).

The artwork was generated with the built-in image generation tool. Prompt direction: Original restrained flat 2D pigment artwork; ultramarine calligraphic open asymmetric loop with a flowing extension and a fine charcoal arc on ivory uncoated paper; abstract thought and dialogue, a central focal composition with generous safe space. No letters, mascot, glass, plastic, glow, 3D objects, or interface elements.

Source: `assets/26.10/palmi-abstract-art.png`. Layout and localized typography: `creative.html`. Reproduce with `render-release.js`. Fonts are Source Serif 4 and Noto Serif SC, distributed under SIL Open Font License 1.1; licenses and source URLs are in `fonts/`.
